Sorting, search, pinned notes and drag & drop.

- Sort notes by last modification, creation date or a custom order.
- Search notes by title and content from the app bar.
- Pin notes to keep them at the top (also in the widget).
- Drag a single selected note to reorder it in custom-order mode.
