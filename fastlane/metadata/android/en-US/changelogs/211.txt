WebDAV support:
- Sync notes with any WebDAV server (Nextcloud, Synology, self-hosted) besides kDrive.
- Same encryption, conflict handling and offline-first behaviour on both.
- Activating one backend removes the other configuration.
- Archive: the "Delete permanently" label is now just "Delete", and swiping a note left moves it to the trash.
- The home widget is no longer rebuilt on every keystroke (less battery).
