kMemo now runs on Linux, with a portable AppImage.

• Pull the note list down to sync.
• Editing one note on two devices keeps both versions instead of losing one.
• Deleting a note from the trash deletes it on every device, instead of coming back.
• Colour, background and archive changes now reach the other device.
• The app opens even when the system keyring is locked.
• Settings copies a sync log when a note does not arrive.
