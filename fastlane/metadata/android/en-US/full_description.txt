kMemo is an offline-first, block-based note-taking app built with Flutter and local SQLite (Drift).

Notes are made of independent text blocks and checklists, so you can capture ideas, tasks and long notes in the same place. The app works fully offline: every note is stored locally on your device.

Features:
- Block-based editor with text and checklist blocks.
- Light, dark and system themes, with custom colors per note.
- Archive and trash, with restore and permanent deletion.
- Home screen widget (Android) showing your latest notes.
- Optional remote sync with kDrive (Infomaniak), including optional client-side AES-256 encryption.
- Link previews (Open Graph) with an explicit opt-in fetch; nothing is fetched automatically.
- Export to .docx and share notes through the system share sheet.
- Six languages: Portuguese, English, French, German, Italian and Spanish.
- No telemetry and no ads.
